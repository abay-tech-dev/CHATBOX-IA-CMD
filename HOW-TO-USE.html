2Choose how you want to use it

A · OBS only (easiest)

  • No account needed
  • Shows chat, subs, gift subs, bits and raids
  • Doesn't show follows and donations

B · StreamElements

  • Free StreamElements account
  • Shows everything, follows and donations included
  • 4 copy-pastes, once

AOBS only, no account

  1. Double-click index.html. It opens in your web browser: this is the configurator.
  2. At the top, type your Twitch channel name (just the name, like your_channel) and click Connect. When people talk in your chat, the messages appear in the preview.
  3. Change the look with the options on the left: colors, text size, alerts, goal bar… Use the test buttons to see alerts.
  4. Click Copy the OBS link. Your channel and all your settings are inside this link.
  5. Open OBS Studio. In the Sources box, click + › Browser › give it a name › OK.
  6. In URL, delete what's there and paste your link with Ctrl + V. Set the Width and Height shown in the configurator, then click OK.
  7. Drag and resize the chat on your scene. You're live!

BStreamElements: the 4 copy-pastes

  1. Go to streamelements.com and log in with your Twitch account.
  2. Open Streaming tools > Overlays > New overlay (choose 1920 x 1080).
  3. Click the + button (Add widget) > Static / Custom > Custom widget.
  4. Select the new widget, then click "Open editor" in the left panel.
  5. You now see tabs: HTML, CSS, JS, FIELDS, DATA. For each of the 4 files below: click the tab, select everything already inside (Ctrl + A), then paste (Ctrl + V). Leave the DATA tab as it is.
  6. Click Done, then Save (top right). The settings of the chat appear in the left panel.
  7. Resize the widget box where you want the chat on your overlay.
  8. Copy your overlay URL (link button at the top), then in OBS: + › Browser › paste it › 1920 x 1080.

?Something doesn't work?

The chat stays empty

Check the channel name (no spaces, no link, just the name). The chat only shows new messages: ask a friend to type something, or use the test buttons in the configurator.

Follows or donations don't show

The OBS-only method can't see follows and donations (Twitch doesn't share them without an account). Use the StreamElements method (B) to get them.

It worked, then it's blank in OBS

You probably moved or renamed the folder. Open index.html again from the new place and copy a new OBS link.

I pasted and nothing appears in StreamElements

Make sure each file went into the right tab and that you replaced everything that was there before (Ctrl + A first). Then click Done and Save.

The text is too small or too big

Change the "Text size" setting. The title bar, goal and prompt grow with it.

Donations show in the wrong currency

Change "Currency for donations": Dollar by default, Euro, Pound, or Automatic (your StreamElements currency).

Does it work on Kick or YouTube?

No, this widget reads Twitch chat only.
